What BRA computes while it thinks

Claim ledger

The answer is broken into single propositions. Each carries its own S.

Decision stability  S

S = P(effect > τ | evidence). τ is the change this literature treats as evidence of regulation.

Epistemic capacity  C

C = |ln[(1−β)/α]| is the largest change in belief this design can justify. It is fixed before any data are collected.

0 C = — 10 nats
0.05
99%

Move α across its range and C swings; move power across all of its and C barely shifts. Capacity is necessary but not sufficient: a design large enough to carry the claim still needs a result stable enough to act on, which is what S decides.
