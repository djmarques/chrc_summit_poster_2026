What BRA computes while it thinks

Claim ledger

The answer is broken into single propositions. Each carries its own S.

Decision stability  S

S = P(true change > τ | data). τ is the change this literature treats as evidence of regulation.

Epistemic capacity  C

C = |ln[(1−β)/α]| is the largest change in belief this design can justify. It is fixed before any data are collected.
